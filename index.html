NumBolt

Mental math game for Android

Math questions fall toward the ground while the clock runs. Tap the right answer out of four before the question lands. Every right answer adds time, a wrong one takes 5 seconds away, and five in a row brings down lightning. Questions start with addition and move on to multiplication, division and two-step problems.
